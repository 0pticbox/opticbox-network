DISTORTION HELP

Live video hot cues, effects, logo layers, detached controls, and recording.

QUICK START

  1. Upload one song and one or more video files.
  2. Select a video from the media library.
  3. Hover across the timeline to jump instantly to that moment.
  4. Hold Shift and click the timeline to add a new hot cue at that starting point.
  5. Press Q–V cue keys while the song keeps playing. The selected default transition fires automatically.
  6. Use T, Y, U, I, O, and P for manual transitions.
  7. Hold the labeled distortion keys for effects, or switch effects into latch mode.
  8. Add a logo, move it directly on the video, then record the performance.
Recommended starting point: Master 45%, Reactivity 35%, White Flash as the default cue transition, Snap Decay 0.12 seconds, Logo 90% opacity.

HOT CUES

Hovering previews and, when enabled, immediately continues playback from the hovered position. A normal click jumps playback without saving. Hold Shift and click to add a new cue in the selected empty slot or the next available key.

Use DESELECT CUE to remove the menu selection without deleting any saved cue point.

Keys: Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V

Continue: starts at the cue and keeps playing. Hold: plays while the key is held, then returns. Freeze: pauses on release. Loop: repeats two seconds from the cue.

DISTORTION EFFECTS

Keys 1–9 trigger the main distortion bank. Backslash triggers the hardest negative-strobe effect. Use HOLD for momentary hits or LATCH to keep effects active.

1Datamosh Drift
2Mirror Shards
3Mirror Grid
4Color Crush
5Split Zoom+
6Block Detonator
7Video Tear
8Negative Pulse+
9Color Surge
\Negative Strobe

All numbered effects now use a universal pass-by-pass stack. Any combination—including Mirror Grid + Negative Pulse—mixes the same way regardless of which key was pressed first. Use CLEAR ALL FX for an instant clean signal. DESTROY SIGNAL generates a randomized stack.

TRANSITIONS

Transitions can be fired manually or used automatically whenever a hot cue jumps to a new video moment.

TWhite Flash
YImpact Shake
UZoom Punch
IFrame Freeze
OSpin Cut
PBlack Drop

Default Cue Transition: choose the transition that fires every time a saved Q–V hot cue is triggered. White Flash is the initial default. Choose None — Hard Cut to disable automatic cue transitions.

Custom mapping: click a key box, then press a replacement key. Scene keys and distortion keys cannot be overwritten. RESET TO T–P MAP restores the original row.

Impact Strength controls how forcefully the transition hits. Snap Decay controls how long the hit lasts.

LOGO LAYER

Upload PNG, WebP, or JPG. Drag the logo directly on the output. Use opacity and size controls. Enable “Distort logo with video” to send it through the active signal effects.

DETACHED CONTROLS

Press DETACH CONTROLS and allow the browser pop-up. Move that window to another monitor. The original page continues rendering and recording the final output.

Keep the original performance page open. The detached panel sends cue, effect, transport, panic, and recording commands back to it.

RECORDING

Select the frame shape, then choose any MP4 or WebM encoder supported by your browser. Unsupported choices are hidden automatically.

Maximum Quality keeps the full canvas resolution at 60 FPS and requests the highest recording bitrate. Browser recording still requires a video codec, so the result is minimally compressed rather than mathematically uncompressed.

For reliable audio recording, press PLAY once before starting the recording so the browser can activate its audio engine.

TROUBLESHOOTING

  • Buttons do nothing: click once inside the page and check that no browser dialog is blocking input.
  • No detached window: allow pop-ups for this page.
  • No recording audio: start the song once before recording and use Chrome or Edge.
  • Video is lagging: reduce Master Distortion, disable Feedback, or use a lower-resolution video.
  • Keyboard shortcuts type into controls: click the video output to return focus to performance mode.
  • Logo has a background: use a transparent PNG or WebP.
