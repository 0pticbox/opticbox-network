DISTORTION HELP

Live video hot cues, effects, logo layers, detached controls, and recording.

QUICK START

  1. Upload one song and one or more video files.
  2. Select a video from the media library.
  3. Hover across the timeline to jump instantly to that moment.
  4. Hold Shift and click the timeline to add a new hot cue at that starting point.
  5. Press Q–V cue keys while the song keeps playing.
  6. Hold the labeled distortion keys for effects, or switch effects into latch mode.
  7. Add a logo, move it directly on the video, then record the performance.
Recommended starting point: Master 45%, Reactivity 35%, Snap Decay 0.12 seconds, Logo 90% opacity.

HOT CUES

Hovering previews and, when enabled, immediately continues playback from the hovered position. A normal click jumps playback without saving. Hold Shift and click to add a new cue in the selected empty slot or the next available key.

Use DESELECT CUE to remove the menu selection without deleting any saved cue point.

Keys: Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V

Continue: starts at the cue and keeps playing. Hold: plays while the key is held, then returns. Freeze: pauses on release. Loop: repeats two seconds from the cue.

DISTORTION EFFECTS

Keys Y U I O H J K L and 7–0 trigger the original bank. The new extreme bank uses [ ] ; ' , . / and backslash. Use HOLD for momentary hits or LATCH to keep effects active.

Extreme effects: Mirror Grid, Vortex Shredder, Random Slicer, Color Crush, Infinite Tunnel, Split Zoom, Block Detonator, and Negative Strobe.

Effects can stack. Use CLEAR ALL FX for an instant clean signal. DESTROY SIGNAL generates a randomized stack.

LOGO LAYER

Upload PNG, WebP, or JPG. Drag the logo directly on the output. Use opacity and size controls. Enable “Distort logo with video” to send it through the active signal effects.

DETACHED CONTROLS

Press DETACH CONTROLS and allow the browser pop-up. Move that window to another monitor. The original page continues rendering and recording the final output.

Keep the original performance page open. The detached panel sends cue, effect, transport, panic, and recording commands back to it.

RECORDING

Select 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1 before recording. The canvas output, song audio, logo, transitions, and active effects are captured into a WebM file.

For reliable audio recording, press PLAY once before starting the recording so the browser can activate its audio engine.

TROUBLESHOOTING

  • Buttons do nothing: click once inside the page and check that no browser dialog is blocking input.
  • No detached window: allow pop-ups for this page.
  • No recording audio: start the song once before recording and use Chrome or Edge.
  • Video is lagging: reduce Master Distortion, disable Feedback, or use a lower-resolution video.
  • Keyboard shortcuts type into controls: click the video output to return focus to performance mode.
  • Logo has a background: use a transparent PNG or WebP.
